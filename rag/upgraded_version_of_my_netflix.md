# Upgraded Version of My Netflix

## What is My Netflix
The original concept of "My Netflix" was a simple gallery application to display family photos and videos, lacking the immersive and engaging experience of modern streaming platforms.

## What is Nukriti+
Nukriti+ is the upgraded, premium evolution of that concept. It transforms a basic gallery into a cinematic streaming service dedicated entirely to personal family memories, trips, birthdays, achievements, and milestones.

## Improvements
- **Netflix-inspired UX**: A dark-themed, cinematic UI with immersive animations and layout.
- **Firestore**: Robust metadata management and real-time updates.
- **Cloudflare R2**: Cost-effective, scalable storage for high-quality video files.
- **HLS Streaming**: Adaptive video playback for smooth viewing on any device.
- **Continue Watching**: Track progress and resume videos right where you left off.
- **Timeline Navigation**: Browse memories chronologically by year.
- **Family Originals**: Featured content collections highlighted on the home page.
- **Admin Dashboard**: Secure management interface for uploading and curating content.
- **Search**: Global search capabilities across all metadata.
- **AI Ready Architecture**: Foundation laid for future AI integrations (search, facial recognition).
- **Future Recommendation Engine**: Smart suggestions for family viewing.
- **Future Face Recognition**: Automated tagging.
- **Future Memory Timeline Generation**: AI-curated highlight reels.
